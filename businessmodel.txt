The website in your screenshot, **airdrops.io**, is a **crypto airdrop discovery and tracking platform**.

Its primary purpose is to help cryptocurrency users discover blockchain projects that are distributing free or reward-based tokens through activities such as testnets, quests, referrals, staking, social engagement, or early adoption.

## Business Model

The website is not just a blog—it's essentially a **directory + news + affiliate marketing platform**.

It makes money through:

* Sponsored airdrop listings
* Banner advertisements
* Affiliate partnerships (wallets, exchanges, crypto products)
* Referral commissions
* Featured project placements
* SEO traffic from Google
* Crypto community traffic (X, Telegram, Discord)

## Why It Ranks Well

Its SEO strategy combines several strengths:

### Programmatic SEO

It has hundreds or thousands of project pages.

Example URLs:

```text
/project/monetrix

/project/lab

/project/arcus
```

Each page targets a unique search intent.

---

### Fresh Content

The homepage and sitemap update frequently.

New projects are added almost every day, which encourages search engines to crawl the site regularly.

## If You Wanted to Build Something Similar

You would be building a **content platform combined with a structured directory**, not just a blog.

The core architecture would include:

```text
Home

↓

Directory

↓

Individual Listings

↓

Categories

↓

Blog

↓

Guides

↓

Search

↓

User Accounts (optional)

↓

Admin CMS

↓

API Layer
```

This pattern is also applicable outside crypto. The same architecture can power:

* Job portals
* Business directories
* SaaS tool directories
* AI tool directories
* Recruitment agency listings
* Automotive listings
* Real estate portals
* Course directories

The key idea is combining **structured listings**, **editorial content**, and **programmatic SEO** to attract consistent organic traffic while providing users with searchable, up-to-date information.
