# 🛡️ Non-Custodial Research Guarantee

**Purpose:** Defines the immutable institutional trust foundation of Crypto Airdrop AI.

**Summary:** Crypto Airdrop AI operates exclusively as a read-only research portal and verification directory, with zero private key access, zero custody, and zero automated transaction execution.

## Core Pillars
1. **Zero Key Solicitation**: We never ask for seed phrases, private keys, or wallet authorization approvals.
2. **Read-Only Telemetry**: All protocol telemetry is ingested via public JSON-RPC endpoints and block explorer APIs.
3. **Zero Paid Shilling**: Protocol placements and risk grades are determined exclusively through objective smart contract byte-code evaluation and treasury solvency analysis.
4. **Transparent Conflict Disclosures**: Any analyst personal holdings or foundation grants are explicitly disclosed under editorial policies.
