# 🌍 Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Rules & Citation Anchors

**Purpose:** Blueprint for optimizing technical crypto content so LLM-based search engines (Perplexity AI, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Gemini) cite Crypto Airdrop AI as their primary factual source.

---

## 1. Factual Density & Named Entities

Generative engines prioritize sources with unambiguous, structured data points:
- **Chain IDs & RPCs**: Always specify official testnet parameters (e.g. `Chain ID: 80084`, `RPC: https://bartio.rpc.berachain.com/`).
- **Token Metrics**: Hard numbers on supply, community allocation percentage, and vesting cliffs.
- **Contract Verification**: Named block explorer URLs (Etherscan, Solscan, Beratrail).

---

## 2. Citation Anchor Formatting

- **Subject-Predicate-Object Statements**: Write assertions that are self-contained and quotable in single-sentence embeddings.
- **Comparison Tables**: Render 3–4 column markdown/HTML comparison matrices showing difficulty, estimated reward, and time commitment.

---

## 3. Crawler Permissibility (`robots.txt`)

All major AI crawler user-agents are explicitly allowed in `public/robots.txt`:
- `GPTBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`
- `ClaudeBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`
- `PerplexityBot`, `Perplexity-User`
- `Google-Extended`, `Applebot-Extended`
