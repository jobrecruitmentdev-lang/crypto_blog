# 🤖 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) & Google AI Overviews Standard

**Purpose:** Rules and patterns for engineering web content to dominate Google AI Overviews (SGE), Perplexity answer cards, and ChatGPT Search results.

---

## 1. The 40–60 Word Direct Answer Box (`tldr`)

- **Placement**: Directly below the `<h1>` headline, above the fold, wrapped in an authoritative callout box.
- **Word Count**: Strictly **40 to 60 words**.
- **Syntax**: Direct Subject-Predicate-Object sentences without filler phrases (*"In this guide...", "Welcome to..."*).
- **Core Formula**:
  1. *Sentence 1*: Exact definition of what the token/protocol is and what the airdrop rewards.
  2. *Sentence 2*: The primary qualifying actions (e.g. liquidity provision, testnet interaction, staking).
  3. *Sentence 3*: Snapshot timing, anti-sybil requirement, or reward distribution mechanic.

---

## 2. Key Takeaways Component

- Placed immediately beneath the Quick Answer Box.
- Exactly 4 bullet points formatted with strong lead-in anchors:
  * 🔹 **Architecture**: Core technical mechanism.
  * 🔹 **Farming Action**: Highest-weighted on-chain activity.
  * 🔹 **Anti-Sybil Defense**: Operational precaution.
  * 🔹 **Security Warning**: Phishing avoidance or allowance revocation.

---

## 3. Semantic Heading Hierarchy

- **H1**: High-CTR target keyword title (< 58 characters).
- **H2**: Section headers matching conversational search queries (e.g. *"Step-by-Step Testnet Action Checklist"*).
- **H3**: Specific dApp, protocol, or sub-task names.
